Ask a model on this PC

The documents and the code are the truth; the index is the way in. ContextMAX finds where your question leads (values, passages, functions, named numbers in the code), opens each of those files at that place, and has a model that runs on this PC read them one passage at a time: what the passage says, in its exact words, and what it points to, which is then read too. Every quote is looked up in the file; one the file does not hold is left out. The answer comes only from the quotes that hold, and ends with its confidence beside ContextMAX's own. Nothing leaves the PC.

The model reads up to nine passages, one at a time: this takes from a few seconds to a minute or two.
